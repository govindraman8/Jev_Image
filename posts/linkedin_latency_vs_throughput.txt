Laya beats Jev on speed. That is true for one decision, and false for a thousand.

I tested both this week. Laya is the open, local clone of TypeSafe's Jev decision model, and it runs on a laptop for free. I ran it three ways on my M3 Pro: PyTorch, MLX, and Core ML on the Neural Engine.

One decision at a time, Laya wins:
- Laya: 6 ms, no network, no bill.
- Jev: 161 ms, because every call crosses the internet.

That is why Laya wins the viral game races. Tetris and Snake ask one question at a time, so latency decides. But the game code does the thinking. One Tetris demo says so in its own source: "The model cannot count or compare, so the arithmetic happens here and it gets the conclusion in words." Snake demos write the answer into the options: "Safe. Best route to food." In every head-to-head I found where someone removed the network delay, Jev chose as well or better.

Real work looks different. Sort a million emails. Score a lead list. Classify a dataset. That is bulk work.

I ran 256 support emails with 6 questions each:
- Jev: 746 decisions per second, every email sent to the right team.
- Laya, fastest setup: 178 per second, 55% right.
- Laya, most accurate setup: 69 per second, 82% right.

The mechanism, measured. With 1 question per email, Jev sorted 99 emails a second and Laya 65. With 6 questions per email, Jev sorted 102 a second and Laya 11. Jev answers every question in one request at once. Laya re-reads the whole email for each question, so 6 questions cost 6 times as much.

At those rates, 100,000 emails with 6 questions each take Jev about 15 minutes and about $2.60. Laya on my laptop takes about 2.4 hours, costs nothing, and gets 82% right. Free is real. So is the wait.

None of this is hidden. Laya's own model card has an "Honest Limits" section. It says the base model is near chance on structured decisions without fine-tuning. The viral clips dropped that asterisk.

So pick by the shape of the job:
- One decision, right now, private data: run Laya locally.
- Thousands of decisions, accurate, today: use Jev.

Laya is great. It is just not magic.
