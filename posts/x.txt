--- 2 ---
Jev is TypeSafe's decision model on OpenRouter. You send a situation and typed questions. It returns a choice and a confidence.

First it composed:
- bright clear day, 69% sure
- two or three happy little clouds, 94%
- a second mountain, because everybody needs a friend, 72%

--- 3 ---
Then it painted. Each pixel: pick one hex code from 18 paints. Titanium White, Phthalo Blue, Van Dyke Brown.

512 requests. 24.9M input tokens. 99.19% of pixels landed right.

The corner reads JEV in Bright Red.

--- 4 ---
My first version asked for the amount of red, green and blue in each pixel.

Jev painted the white background green. It read "amount of red" as "is this a red thing?"

Accuracy: 19%. I renamed the channels R, G, B and restructured the brief. Accuracy hit 97%.

--- 5 ---
The honest part: my code drew the geometry. Each request told Jev which colours sit in that row.

Jev chose the composition and every pixel's paint. It did not imagine the mountain.

Given only plain English, it paints a blur.

--- 6 ---
Jev costs $0.042 per million input tokens. It made 65,536 decisions in under a minute and got 99% right.

Painting is a toy. Routing, triage and classification at that price are not.

Model: typesafe/jev-1.13 on OpenRouter.
